Stika

The sticker and notes app to meet all your journaling needs.

Make your notes aesthetic with cute stickers extracted using Apple's own VisionKit.

Features

  • Drag and drop images into the app to create sticker sheets or notes
  • Drag and drop stickers or notes directly from the app to your desired note-taking app
