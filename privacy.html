Privacy policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Stika is made by WaoStudios. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Your images stay on your device

Every image, sticker, sticker sheet, and note you make in Stika is stored on your device. Stika does not upload them to the cloud or send them to WaoStudios or anyone else.

Information we collect

None. Stika does not collect personal information, and it does not require an account.

How stickers are made

Stika extracts stickers from your images using Apple's VisionKit. This processing happens on your device.

Sharing

Your content leaves Stika only when you drag a sticker or note into another app. What that app does with it is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Children

Because Stika collects no information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email your@email.com.
