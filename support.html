Questions

How do I make a sticker?

Drag an image into Stika. The app uses Apple's VisionKit to lift the subject out of the image so you can use it as a sticker on a sticker sheet or a note.

How do I use my stickers in another app?

Drag a sticker or note from Stika and drop it into your note-taking app.

How do I request a feature?
